# RezervAz

## Overview

RezervAz is an all-in-one online booking and appointment management platform designed for service-based businesses in Azerbaijan. It allows customers to book appointments easily while helping businesses manage schedules, staff, services, and reservations from a single platform.

The long-term vision is to become Azerbaijan's leading appointment booking ecosystem for every business that operates by appointments or reservations.

---

# Vision

To simplify appointment booking for both customers and businesses by providing a modern, reliable, and easy-to-use platform that saves time, reduces missed appointments, and improves business efficiency.
